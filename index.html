The Path2Safe method

Four steps from storm to safe

The plan we're designing follows your family before, during and after an earthquake, storm, outage or wildfire.

  1. P

    Step 1 · Before

    Plan

    Contacts, meeting places, and your schools' and workplaces' own procedures, written for your household.

  2. A

    Step 2 · Before

    Assemble

    Supplies sized to the people and pets in your home, at home and in the car.

  3. T

    Step 3 · During

    Travel

    Safe routes to each other, with backup routes when roads or bridges close.

  4. H

    Step 4 · After

    Home

    Reunite, shelter in place and recover. We refresh the plan each year as life changes.
