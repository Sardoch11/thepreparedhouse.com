Sources

Liquefaction: DOGAMI O-19-09 (2019) via Multnomah County NHMP Hazards Data, layer 3 (classes 4–5).

Landslides: DOGAMI Statewide Landslide Information Database (SLIDO 4.5), mapped deposits.

Flooding: FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer, special flood hazard areas (1% annual chance); floodways weighted higher.

Emergency transportation routes: RDPO and Metro regional update. Seismic lifelines: ODOT Oregon Seismic Lifeline Routes (tiers 1–3).

Wildland-urban interface: Oregon Department of Forestry. Oregon repealed its statewide wildfire hazard map in 2025, so the app doesn't show hazard ratings.

BEECN sites: Portland Bureau of Emergency Management, PortlandMaps open data.

Bridges: Multnomah County bridge program; KGW summary of agency reports. Positions approximate.

Street routing: OSRM via FOSSGIS (routing.openstreetmap.de). Map © OpenStreetMap contributors.
